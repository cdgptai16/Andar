Before your first ride

Passengers only. The cues are meant for someone who is not driving. Never look at or tap this phone while you drive.

This is a personal experiment, not a medical device. If your sickness is severe or getting worse, talk to your doctor.

Your ride data stays on this phone. Destination searches go to OpenStreetMap's public map services.

2 · Where are you going?

With a destination, cues arrive before each turn. Without one, you still get cues for braking and turning as they happen.

3 · How is the phone mounted?

Put it where you can see it from the corner of your eye while looking out the window. It must not move during the ride.

4 · Start

Stay still for 2 seconds after tapping start, so the phone can learn which way is down.
